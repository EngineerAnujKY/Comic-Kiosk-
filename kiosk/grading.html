Grading guide

How grading works

Collectors describe a comic's condition with a number from 0.5 to 10.0. Higher means a cleaner, flatter, sharper copy. This store uses a CGC-style 10-point scale for the copies sold here, and every price on the site is for the grade you choose. Grade 9.8 is the listed price, and lower grades cost less.

Listings here are sample data for a demo project. They follow a CGC-style scale but are not CGC-certified copies.

The full scale

Other grades on a 10-point scale include:

  • 10.0 Gem Mint
  • 9.9 Mint
  • 9.6 Near Mint+
  • 9.2 Near Mint-
  • 9.0 Very Fine/Near Mint
  • 8.5 Very Fine+
  • 7.0 Fine/Very Fine
  • 5.0 Very Good/Fine
  • 2.0 Good
  • 1.0 Fair
  • 0.5 Poor

Which grade should I pick?

  • Building a shelf on a budget: 6.0 or 8.0 copies look good and cost far less.
  • Collecting for value: 9.4 and 9.8 copies are the ones collectors chase.
  • Just want to read it: 4.0 or 6.0 gives you the whole story at the lowest price.
